## Why this file exists

Your grade is credited from the repository's **collaborator list**, not from
this table. So this table cannot give someone credit they would not otherwise
get.

What it does is catch the mistake while it is still cheap: if someone is
written here but cannot push, they were never invited, and there are still
ninety minutes in which to fix that. Discovering it after grading is a much
worse conversation.
