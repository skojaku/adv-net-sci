\question{1}{10}{Build a BCDE graph.}

A connected graph is a \textbf{BCDE graph} if, under each of the four
centrality measures below, exactly one node is the most central, and these four
nodes are four different nodes of the graph:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=0.1em, topsep=0.3em]
  \item[\textbf{B}] betweenness centrality
  \item[\textbf{C}] closeness centrality
  \item[\textbf{D}] degree centrality
  \item[\textbf{E}] eigenvector centrality
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3em}
Draw a BCDE graph with \textbf{at most 12 nodes}. The graph must be connected,
with no self-loops and no multiple edges. Number the nodes.

\vspace{0.3em}
Then, for each of the four measures, give the most central node.
