% Quiz 04 --- The CCDF and the friendship paradox. Printed, worked in class,
% 20 minutes. Two pages: the questions and the table on page 1, four copies of
% the same log--log graph paper on page 2 (spares, in case a plot goes wrong).
%
% Students are given the degree counts of a 10,000-person network and draw two
% CCDFs by hand on log--log paper: the person's degree, then the friend's. The
% table has ten rows, degrees 1, 2, 4, ..., 512, and nobody has any other
% degree, so every number on the sheet is exact.
%
% The sheet gives the definition of a friend (a random end of a random edge)
% but NOT the route: the k n_k weighting, the slope 1 - gamma and the friend
% slope 2 - gamma are what the student has to produce. Do not put them back.
%
% Compile with: xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode quiz04.tex   (run twice)
