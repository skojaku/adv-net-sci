## Pen-and-Paper Exercise: Why is our social network small world?

Do these *before* the lecture --- they are designed so that you discover the
concepts yourself, with nothing but a pencil.

- ✍️ [Six Handshakes to a Stranger](../../pen-and-papers/m02/six-handshakes/exercise.pdf): distance, average path length, diameter, and what two long-range friendships do to a village.
- ✍️ [Do Your Friends Know Each Other?](../../pen-and-papers/m02/do-your-friends-know-each-other/exercise.pdf): triangles, clustering, and why any clustering number is meaningless without a random baseline.
- ✍️ [It's a small world!! 6 degrees of separation](http://estebanmoro.org/pdf/netsci_for_kids/6_degrees_of_separation.pdf) [@esteban-moro-worksheet]

## Pen-and-Paper Exercise: after the lecture

Do this one *after* the lecture --- it takes the same town and pushes it until
the machinery breaks.

- ✍️ [Turning the Dial](../../pen-and-papers/m02/turning-the-dial/exercise.pdf): rewiring a ring lattice by hand, deriving how clustering decays with $p$, and finding out that a plain lattice passes the $\sigma > 1$ test.
