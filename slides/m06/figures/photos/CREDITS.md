## Notes

- CC BY and CC BY-SA require the credit line to stay with the image. CC BY-SA applies to adaptations (the crop), so the crop carries the same licence; the slide deck itself is not an adaptation of the photo.
- Crops: `caesar.jpg` full width, 40 px of margin above the head removed; `augustus.jpg` full width, plinth removed (the pointing hand and both feet stay); `milliarium.jpg` centred on the carved frieze; `aesop.jpg` is the top two thirds of the canvas.
- The fragments in `milliarium.jpg` are the ones the Forum labels "Milliarium Aureum". Whether they belong to the milestone is doubtful (see the report): a round marble base or plinth with an anthemion (palmette) frieze, about 3 m across by Richardson's estimate. Do not caption them "the milestone", caption them "remains traditionally identified as the Milliarium Aureum".
- Alternative photo of the same fragments with the label stone fully readable ("MILLIARIVM AVREVM"): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RomaForoRomanoMiliariumAureum.JPG (2048 x 1536, CC BY-SA 3.0, author not given in the metadata, so it was not used).
