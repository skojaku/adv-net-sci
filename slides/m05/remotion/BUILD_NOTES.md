# Building a slide here

The plan is `DECK_SPEC.md` (one row per stage). `src/slides/S04.tsx` is the reference slide (its colours are the new palette): read it
first. It shows the stage structure, the fades, a plot, KaTeX, and how a stage's animation ends
before its mark.

## What a slide is

- One React component per slide, `src/slides/SNN.tsx`, plus `export const marks = [...]`.
  `marks[i]` is the frame where stage `i` is finished. Stage 0 plays when the slide opens; each
  click plays the next stage and stops at its mark. Going back plays in reverse.
- The picture at `marks[i]` is what the room looks at, so **every animation of stage i ends at or
  before `marks[i]`**, and **the last stage is a complete picture** (going back to the slide lands on it).
- A stage is 30 to 60 frames (30 fps). A stage that runs through many items (a sweep) may take up to 90.
- Frame numbers come only from `useCurrentFrame()`. No `useState`, no `Date.now`, no `Math.random`,
  no `useIdle`. Randomness comes from `src/data/data.ts` (fixed seeds).
- `interpolate` and `prog(frame, a, b)` need `a < b`.
- Heavy computation goes at module level, once.

## Helpers

- `src/lib/anim.ts`: `prog` (ease-out 0 to 1), `smooth`, `lin`, `betweenStages(frame, marks, from, to)`
  (opacity of something shown in stages from..to), `fromStage` (appears at a stage, stays),
  `caption`, `reveal`, `piecewise`, `stepped`. `stageStart(marks, i)` is the first frame of stage i.
- `src/components/Frame.tsx`: `Frame` (page number; pass `n`; optional `title` + rule, normally none; `zoom` and `top` move and enlarge the content) and `Part` (section divider).
- `src/components/Fade.tsx`: `Fade` (an HTML layer with opacity and slide-up), `Canvas` (an SVG at canvas
  coordinates), `FadeG`.
- `src/components/Text.tsx`: `Box` (positioned text), `Cap` (grey handwriting caption), `Tag` (number tag),
  `Term` (key term: red, bold). `src/components/Tex.tsx`: `Tex` (KaTeX).
- `src/lib/network.tsx`: `Network` (nodes and edges at canvas coordinates; per-node fill, ring, label),
  `mix` (colour blend), `toCanvas` (unit box onto a region).
- `src/lib/ring.tsx`: the ring of triangles. `src/lib/metrics.ts`: entropy, mutual information, NMI, pair counts,
  Rand, expected Rand, ARI, and the eight-node example (`TRUTH8`, `FOUND8`, `ALONE8`).
- `src/lib/sbm.ts` and `src/components/BlockTable.tsx`: the 8-node block model of S18 to S23 (its circle layout is `arcs8` in `src/lib/sbmLayout.ts`).
- `src/data/data.ts`: generated by `scripts/make_data.py`; never edit by hand. Karate club (`KARATE_*`),
  the random network (`NOISE_*`), the 30-dot shuffles (`SHUFFLES`, `SHUFFLE_RAND`, `SHUFFLE_ARI`, `EXPECTED_RAND`),
  the SBM's fixed uniform numbers (`SBM_U`).

Do not change an existing shared file to suit one slide. Put a slide's own helper in a new file next to it
(`src/lib/<name>.ts(x)`).

## Style (the Marp deck at 1.5x)

- Canvas 1920 x 1080. Lay a slide out in the region x 120 to 1800, y 190 to 990 as before; with no title `Frame` moves it up by `zoom * top - 50` and enlarges it (default 1.08), so it ends up in y 50 to about 900. The band below y = 920 stays empty for subtitles; `scripts/check_bottom.py` enforces it. Slides with room use a larger `zoom` (S02, S09, S11 to S18, S26, S34), crowded ones a smaller one (S05, S07). The page number comes from `Frame`.
- Images go in `src/assets/` and are imported (`import png from '../assets/x.png'`, then `<Img src={png} />`; S33, S38), so that the single-file build can put them inside the html. `src/components/FormulaStack.tsx` stacks formulas one per stage (newest large, older ones smaller, rows of one group change size together, optional `note` under a row); S29, S35 to S37 use it.
- To look at an animation between the stage ends: `node scripts/frames.mjs <composition id> 0,30,60 <out dir>` renders those frames at half size. A slide that follows another one should open on the picture the previous slide ends with (S13 opens on S12's last picture; S14 opens on S13's table), so that the cut between them is not seen.
- **Colour.** Everything drawn is `C.blue` by default; small notes are `C.soft` grey, black is `C.ink`. **Red (`C.red`) is for
  emphasised text only** (a key term via `Term`, the higher value, "disagree"). Never a fill, a group, a band or a line. No green.
  No bar charts.
- **Groups (communities) are told apart by hue**, never red (`src/lib/look.ts`): `LOOK[0]` blue, `LOOK[1]` orange, `LOOK[2]` brown,
  `LOOK[3]` purple, `LOOK[4]` dark grey. `UNKNOWN` (black) is a node with no group yet or a "?". `HOLLOW` is a plain white node.
  `Network` takes `look` (one or one per node) and `lookTo` + `t` to cross-fade one colouring into another (never blend colours).
  Stripes (brown) are not a group colour: they fill bands (`BAND_HATCH`) and the overlap of the NMI discs. When two colourings
  of the same network are to be compared, draw both at once, side by side (S05, S21).
- **A pair of nodes is never drawn as two nodes joined by a line** (it reads as a network edge). Pairs are the cells of a matrix
  (`src/lib/pairmatrix.tsx`).
- Do not show a random number `u` or any internal step of the computer: say "10 tickets, 9 say edge, draw one".
- Text sizes: body 45, grey notes 40, in-figure labels and axis numbers 36 or more (never below 34), handwriting labels
  (`Cap`, font `F.hand`) 45. A disc is 39 px across or more.
- **Few words.** At most one short caption per stage (about 8 words, 12 at the very most), no sentence repeated in two places, no
  paragraph. Prefer a symbol, a number or a drawing to a sentence. The lecturer speaks the rest. Remove a caption that only restates
  what the picture shows.
- **Name the unit.** The things we group are **nodes**. Say "node" (not dot, vertex or member). The Rand index and ARI count
  **pairs of nodes**; NMI looks at **one node at a time**. Every slide that counts something says what it counts ("pairs of nodes",
  "one node"), and draws that unit (two nodes joined by a line for a pair, one highlighted node for a single node).
- Bold (the `Term` component) marks key terms and nothing else. **No em-dash anywhere** in slide text.
- Voice: plain English, "Let's ..." and "We ...", no rhetoric. **A question slide carries no answer anywhere on it.**
- Do not put a paragraph below a build. Put captions where the stage that introduces them starts.
- Numbers on a slide come from `DECK_SPEC.md` ("検算した数字"), from `src/data/data.ts`, or from the helpers
  in `metrics.ts`. Never type a number from memory. If a number you need is not there, compute it in code and say so.

## Checking a slide

    npx tsc --noEmit                      # type errors (look at your own files only; others may be in progress)
    npx remotion still src/remotion-entry.ts S04-resolution-limit out/s04-56.png --frame=56

The composition id is `S<NN>-<id>` (see `src/slides/index.ts`). Render a still at **every mark** and at a few frames
in the middle of each stage, then look at the PNG. Check: nothing overlaps or leaves the canvas, text is not cut or
wrapped unexpectedly, discs are not touching, the stage's end picture is complete, and a stage's new element is not
already on screen in the previous stage.

`out/` is not committed. Do not run git, `npm install`, or the dev server.

## A single html file for students

`npm run build:single` writes `out/m05-community-detection.html` (about 2.3 MB): the click-through deck with the scripts, styles, fonts and
pictures all inside it, so it opens from the file system with no network (click or the right arrow: next, left arrow: back, `f`: full
screen). `out/` is not committed (a build command produces it), so run the command again after any change. How it differs from `npm run build`:
`scripts/build_single.mjs` swaps `@remotion/google-fonts` for `src/single/fonts-*.ts` (the `@fontsource` woff2 files, inlined), keeps only the
woff2 of the KaTeX and font files, inlines every asset, and pastes the one script and the one stylesheet into the html. Checked from file://
in headless Chrome: the fonts load, the picture shows, no request leaves the file, no script error.

## The narrated video (a separate file; the slides are not touched)

Full guide: `slides/NARRATED_VIDEO_GUIDE.md`. Short version: `node scripts/extract_mechvibes.mjs --pack=cherrymx-red-abs --release=0.5` (once), `npm run video:prose` (the slides'
own sentences, when a slide changes), then `npm run video:audio` and `npm run video` write `out/m05-narrated.mp4` (1920 x 1080, 30 fps, about 26 minutes with sound).
It is one continuous talk (the three section dividers are left out). Each slide plays stage by stage as the click-through deck does; once a stage's animation has finished the
picture is held while a small figure lying down at the bottom (left of centre) types short notes as terminal lines (a `$` prompt, no frame); then the next stage starts. **The slides' sentences are not drawn in the video: they are typed
in the chat** (`ProseContext` in `src/components/Text.tsx`; the deck itself is unchanged). Everything lives in `src/video/` with its own entry point (`src/video/entry.ts`), so `src/slides/`,
`src/Root.tsx` and the player app are unchanged.

- `src/video/narration.ts`: the written lines, `narration[slide number][stage index] = [line, ...]`, which add to the slides' sentences (`'@mirror'` marks where those go). A line is one bubble, at most 64 characters.
- `src/video/prose.json`: the slides' sentences per stage, written by `scripts/collect_prose.mjs` (committed; run `npm run video:prose` after changing a slide's text).
- `src/video/typing.ts`: human-like keystrokes (bursts, pauses, occasional wrong key, backspace, retype); deterministic.
- `src/video/timeline.ts`: when each stage and bubble happens (pure, no React). `NarratedDeck.tsx` plays the slides, `Narrator.tsx` draws the terminal lines (three at most, below y = 920), `Loafer.tsx` the figure.
- Sound: `scripts/make_typing_audio.mjs` writes `out/video-public/typing.wav` from the same keystrokes (shared code in `scripts/lib/typing_sound.mjs`). WAV samples in `sounds/` are used when present (cut from a Mechvibes
  pack by `scripts/extract_mechvibes.mjs`, or your own recordings), otherwise the sound is synthesized. **The space bar is silent** (`--space-sound` to turn it on).
- `npm run video:sampler` then `node scripts/render_video.mjs --id=M05-sampler --out=out/sound-packs.mp4`: a listening test of all ten Mechvibes keyboard packs.
- `node scripts/render_video.mjs --frames=0-400 --out=out/test.mp4` renders a part; `--still=120,900 --dir=out/video-stills` renders single frames.
After editing the narration, `prose.json` or the slides run `npm run video:audio` again before `npm run video`: the timeline (and so the audio) changes.
