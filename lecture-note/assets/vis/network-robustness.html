Twelve towns, twenty cables

Twelve towns and nothing joining them. You have twenty cables. Lay them wherever you like, then knock towns out and watch what is left of the network. Spread the cables evenly, note what happens; pile them onto two or three towns, and note what happens then. Same budget, different survival.

  • Click two towns to lay a cable between them. Click the same two again to take it back and get the cable returned to your budget.
  • Random failure removes towns in a random order. Targeted attack always removes whichever surviving town has the most cables.
  • Connectivity is the share of towns still in one connected piece. R is that share averaged over the whole removal — one number for how well the wiring held.
