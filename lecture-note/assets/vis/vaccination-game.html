Who do you vaccinate?

A hundred and twenty people, a disease that spreads along friendships, and a limited number of doses. Vaccinated people cannot catch it and cannot pass it on. The question is not how many doses you have. It is who you give them to.

  • Random picks people out of the phone book. It needs to know nothing at all.
  • Nominate a friend picks a person at random and then vaccinates one of their friends, chosen at random. It still needs no map of the network — only a question you can ask at the door.
  • Biggest hubs vaccinates the most connected people. It is the best you can do, and it needs the whole network mapped, which in an outbreak nobody has.
