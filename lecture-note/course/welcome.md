Welcome to the course! In this class, we will explore the fascinating world of **networks**—from the social connections that shape our lives to the complex systems that power the internet, biology, and beyond.

The course builds every idea twice: once with your own hands, on paper, and once in code. You do not need any prior network science, and the code you meet starts from basic Python.

## What to Expect

- **Engaging Lectures:** Each week, we'll dive into key concepts in network science, supported by interactive discussions and in-class activities.
- **Hands-on Coding:** You'll work with real network data using Python and modern data science tools. Every module's code lives in a notebook that runs in your browser — nothing to install to follow along.
- **Collaborative Learning:** Participate in group activities, discussions, and our dedicated [Discord server](discord.md) for Q&A and support.
- **Assignments:** A [Pair Notebook](pair-notebook.qmd) for each of the first six modules — a one-on-one session with an AI tutor, graded on your reasoning rather than your code — plus an in-class [group mini-project](mini-project.qmd), a weekly quiz, and a final project.
- **Support:** The instructor, the course Discord for informal questions, and — inside a Pair Notebook session — your own AI tutor.

## How to Get Started

1. **Read the [About](about.md) page** to meet your instructor.
2. **Join the [Discord server](discord.md)** for announcements, help, and community.
3. **[Set up your machine](setup.qmd)** — a terminal, git, and two small tools. About twenty minutes, once.
4. **Read the [Pair Notebook](pair-notebook.qmd) guide** before your first assignment. It is unlike any assignment you have handed in before, and the walkthrough on that page shows you a whole session in a minute.
5. **Check [How this course works](activities.md)** to see what is graded and when.
