title: "Discord"
---

We use a dedicated **Discord server** for this course, for communication, Q&A,
and everything that is easier to sort out in a sentence than in an email.

**Nothing on Discord is graded.** It is for questions, announcements and
community — every graded thing is handed in through Classroom 50, the quiz form,
or Brightspace. Ask half-formed questions there; that is what it is for.

The server is where you can:

- Ask questions about lectures, assignments, and projects
- Discuss concepts and share resources with your peers
- Get help from the instructor and from your classmates
- Find teammates for the mini-project and the final project

**Invitation links to the Discord server will be distributed via Brightspace.**
Please check the Brightspace announcements or course materials for the latest
invite link.

Once you join you will find a handful of channels:

| Channel | For |
|---|---|
| `#welcome` | say hello, and read the pinned messages |
| `#general` | announcements, and anything about the course that is not a question |
| `#qa` | questions: lectures, assignments, setup trouble |
| `#ai-tutor` | the AI tutor — `pi`, your course API key, and anything it got wrong |
| `#random` | everything else, including figures and results you want to show off |

If you're new to Discord, it's a free platform available on web, desktop, and
mobile. If you have any trouble joining, please contact the instructor.
