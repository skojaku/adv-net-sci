\parthead{1}{Seven bridges}

A university campus sits on both banks of a creek. There are two islands in the
middle. Seven footbridges, \emph{a} to \emph{g}, join the four pieces of land:
the north bank \textbf{N}, the south bank \textbf{S}, the west island
\textbf{W}, and the east island \textbf{E}.

You are a campus tour guide. Every footbridge has a donor's name on it, so your
tour must cross \textbf{every bridge exactly once}.
