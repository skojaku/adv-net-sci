\parthead{1}{Seven highways}

Seven highways join four Upstate New York cities: Ithaca, Syracuse,
Binghamton, and Albany.

{\bf Question 1(a)}: Drive every highway exactly once, without lifting your
pencil. Start and finish anywhere; cities may repeat, highways may not. Write
in each circle when you drive that road --- 1 first, then 2, on to 7. Two are
filled in: you start at Ithaca, take NY-13, then NY-34 straight back.

{\bf Question 1(b)}: The same four cities, and an eighth highway:
\textbf{US-11}, which really does run alongside I-81. Number your route again.
Can you drive every highway without lifting your pencil?

{\bf Question 4(c)}: Back to the highway map. Your drive in 1(a) used no road
