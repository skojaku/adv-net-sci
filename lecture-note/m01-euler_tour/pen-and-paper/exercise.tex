a creek, with two small islands in the middle, all stitched together by
footbridges. Every footbridge carries a donor's name on a brass plaque, and the
development office wants a walking tour that crosses \textbf{every footbridge
exactly once}, so that no donor is left out.

Start anywhere, finish anywhere, set foot on the same landmass as often as you
like. Do not cross the same footbridge twice, and do not swim.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white]
Three names for three levels of strictness. A \textbf{walk} is any sequence of
landmasses joined by bridges --- repeat whatever you like. A \textbf{trail}
never repeats a \emph{bridge}. A \textbf{path} never repeats a

get a \textbf{circuit} (a closed trail) or a \textbf{cycle} (a closed path).
\end{tcolorbox}
